# Scope

Status: FROZEN upon merge of PH-PLAN-001.

## NECESSARY MVP
- GEF governance and engineering harness.
- Multi-tenant core, auth/authorization and tenant isolation.
- Secure Polymarket account/session integration.
- Market discovery and real-time market/order-book data.
- Micro Maker Scalper.
- Arbitrage Sentinel.
- Deterministic Risk Engine.
- Order/position/PnL reconciliation and Event Journal.
- Replay and Paper Trading.
- User dashboard and settings.
- Admin dashboard plus tenant/global kill switch.
- Observability, audit and recovery.
- Pilot deployment.
- Geographic eligibility guard.
- Replaceable AI adapter with safe no-AI fallback.

## IMPORTANT AFTER MVP ACCEPTANCE
- Richer AI market intelligence.
- Advanced maker inventory models.
- Cross-market graph analytics.
- Billing/subscriptions.
- Builder attribution/rebate analytics.
- Additional strategy packs through separate Work Orders.

## FUTURE
- Mobile app.
- Additional prediction-market adapters.
- Advanced ML/online learning.
- Multi-region workers.
- Sophisticated portfolio optimization.

## OUT OF SCOPE FOR MVP
- Guaranteed daily profit.
- Martingale or doubling recovery.
- Withdrawal/custody features.
- Geoblock bypass, VPN/proxy routing or jurisdiction evasion.
- Copy trading.
- Leveraged crypto-futures trading.
- Unbounded autonomous LLM trading.
