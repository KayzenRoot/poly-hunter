## Correction delta 3 — CR-07 (independent audit `5427442224`)

That audit **closed CR-05 and CR-06** and **accepted the technical basis of the 25 proposed
dispositions**. Exactly one finding remained, resolved by commit `0a76149`:

- **CR-07 — `@supabase/ssr` 0.12.7 auth-cookie cache headers.** The server contract is
  `setAll(cookiesToSet, cacheHeaders)`, and the library delivers
  `Cache-Control: private, no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate, max-age=0`, `Expires: 0` and
  `Pragma: no-cache` alongside every auth-cookie write. `apps/web/proxy.ts` already forwarded them
  directly; `apps/web/src/identity/supabase-adapter.ts` declared `setAll(cookiesToSet)` and discarded
  the delivered policy. The fix centralizes the policy in a new server-only
  `apps/web/src/identity/auth-cache-headers.ts` (`applyAuthNoStoreHeaders`, frozen constant, no mutable
  module state), makes the adapter declare `setAll(cookiesToSet, cacheHeaders)` and **throw** on
  policy drift instead of silently serving a weaker policy, and applies the policy on every response
  path of `/api/auth/login`, `/auth/callback` and `/api/auth/logout` — including the callback's
  denied / invalid / failure redirects. The boundary is documented in source: the `next/headers`
  cookie store exposes no response handle, so cookies are written by the cookie store and the
  anti-cache headers are obligatorily applied by the Route Handler through the helper. CSRF (CR-02),
  RBAC, `TenantContext` and the open-redirect / trusted-origin behavior (CR-04) are unchanged. 16 new
  tests; suite total 121/121. Receipt:
  `.engineering/evidence/PH-M01-WO-002/receipts/cr07-auth-cache-headers.txt`.
